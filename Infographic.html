The goal of the infrographic project was to investigate a communication technology using a variety of resources. We had to determine the orgin, operating principle,uses,impacts, and possible future of our selected communication technology and synthesize the information into a visual presentation.

The infographic was mean to construct a visual display that represents how the selected communication tehnology fits the communication theory model and enables an understanding of the technology's operating principle.
