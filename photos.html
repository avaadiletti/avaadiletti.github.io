For the photo assignment, we had to take multiple photos of different objects or landscapes so that we could expiriment with Photoshop and use all of the editing tools to enhance the way our photos look.

The purpose of this project was to learn about the different types of editing you can do in Photoshop and use InDesign to showcase the before and after photos. Through this assignment I was able to use the images I took and experiment with things like lighting, resolution, and composition.
